# Review Order

Step 3 of the [[Checkout Flow]]. Shows the shipping address and the payment method: the masked
card, or "Paid with PayPal".
Requires both [[Shipping]] and [[Payment]] to be complete — see [[Payment Rules]].
Placing the order creates exactly one order.
