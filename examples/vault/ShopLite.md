# ShopLite

ShopLite is a small online shop. Customers sign in via [[Login]], land on the [[Dashboard]],
and buy through the [[Checkout Flow]]. They manage their details on the [[Profile]] page and
see their purchases in [[Order History]]. Staff with the [[Admin]] role use the [[Admin Panel]].

Personas: [[Customer]], [[Admin]].

Business rules live in [[Session Rules]] and [[Payment Rules]].
